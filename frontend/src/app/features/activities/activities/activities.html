RECORD WHAT HAPPENED

📝 Farm activity

An activity is something that actually happened on the farm — not something that is only planned.

1

What happened?

Example: field prepared, crop sown, irrigation done, animal fed or medicine given.

2

Where or to whom?

Connect the activity to a field, crop, animal or another farm resource.

3

When?

Keep the actual date and time so Agrova can understand the farm's timeline later.

4

What was used?

Connect seeds, fertilizer, feed, water, machinery or other resources when relevant.

ACTIVITY TYPES

🌾 The work of a real farm

Different farm activities will eventually share the same activity model while remaining connected to their domain.

🌱

Field work

Ploughing, preparation, sowing, transplanting and other field work.

💧

Irrigation

Watering, irrigation events and related field work.

🧪

Input application

Fertilizer, manure and other crop-care inputs applied to a field or crop.

🐄

Animal care

Feeding, treatment, vaccination and other animal-care activities.

🌿

Crop observation

Field observations that capture what the farmer actually saw.

🌾

Harvest

Harvest events that eventually connect to yield and economics.

ACTIVITY MEMORY

An activity is not a task

📋

Task

Something that needs to be done.

"I need to irrigate Field A."
→
🚜

Activity

Something that was actually done.

"Field A was irrigated today."

FARM MEMORY

What happened becomes useful later

Agrova will eventually connect activities with resources, weather, crop stage, animal health, yield, cost and outcomes so the farmer's history can inform future decisions.
