INFORMATION GAPS

🔎 Sometimes Agrova needs more evidence

A good system should know when it does not have enough information instead of guessing.

📷

Photo needed

A crop or animal condition may need a visual observation before it can be understood further.

🧪

Test needed

Soil or other measured evidence may be needed instead of relying only on assumptions.

👨‍🌾

More details needed

Agrova may need to ask the farmer what they observed, where it happened and when.

🧑‍⚕️

Expert needed

Some situations should be escalated to an appropriate professional instead of being guessed by AI.

RECOMMENDATION PRINCIPLE

💡 Explain before suggesting

Recommendations should be connected to evidence, reasoning, alternatives and the farmer's actual constraints.

1

What do we know?

Show the relevant farm observations and verified data.

2

Why does it matter?

Explain the relationship in farmer-friendly language.

3

What are the options?

Present practical alternatives and relevant tradeoffs.

4

What happened after?

Record the eventual action and outcome for farm memory.
