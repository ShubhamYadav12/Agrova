FARM CONTEXT

Weather becomes useful when it is connected to the particular field, crop, crop stage, water situation and activities happening on the farm.

🌾

Field

Connect weather conditions to the actual field where farming work is happening.

🌱

Crop stage

The same weather can matter differently depending on what the crop is currently going through.

💧

Water

Rain and water availability can eventually be understood together instead of separately.

🚜

Activities

Weather can become part of the context around planned and completed farm activities.

AGRICULTURAL RELEVANCE

🌡️

Temperature

WEATHER MEMORY
